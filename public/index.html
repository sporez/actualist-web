Release notes for each build

Your server, not ours

No Actualist account, cloud, or analytics. The app talks only to your Actual server, and to SimpleFIN if you set it up. Credentials stay in the iOS Keychain. Privacy

  1. iPhone & iPad Local copy, works offline
  2. ⇄
  3. Your Actual server Home lab, NAS, or VPS
  4. Actualist cloud Doesn’t exist

Why a native app

  • Opens instantlyReads your saved budget first, syncs in the background.
  • Widgets, Siri & ShortcutsBalances and quick actions without opening the app.
  • Made for iPadSidebar, keyboard entry, up to five months side by side.
  • Native chartsNet worth, cash flow, and spending reports.
