Support

Contact

Email support@actualist.app. Say which iPhone or iPad you use, your iOS version, and your Actualist version (at the bottom of Settings). Never send passwords, encryption keys, or bank details.

A diagnostic report helps find the cause. In Actualist, open Settings → Support → Share Diagnostic Report and attach it. Reports do not include passwords, server addresses, names, or any budget details.

Can't sign in

  • Check that your server's address opens in Safari on the same device.
  • Use your Actual server password, not your budget's encryption password.
  • If your server is only reachable over a VPN or Tailscale, connect to it first.
  • Use an address that starts with https:// for a server outside your home network.

A budget won't open

An encrypted budget needs its separate encryption password. A budget appears in Actualist only after it has been uploaded to your server for sync.

Changes aren't showing on other devices

Actualist saves changes on your device first and uploads them when your server is reachable. Open Settings → Connection & Sync to see whether anything is waiting to upload. Before you erase or replace a device, wait until it shows nothing waiting, because changes that were never uploaded can't be recovered.

Keep a backup

Actualist works with your real budget. Keep an Actual backup of every budget you use with it.

Bugs and feature requests

If you have a GitHub account, you can also open an issue. Report security problems privately as described in SECURITY.md.
